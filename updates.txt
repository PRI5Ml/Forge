HOW TO POST AN UPDATE (this note is not shown in the app)
Put each new update at the TOP, right below this note.
Start an update with ## and a title. Use - at the start of a line for a bullet point.
Plain lines show as normal text.

## Oct 26 — Daily targets for your goal
- Food tab → Calculate my targets: pick lose fat, maintain or build muscle
- Uses your age, height, weight and activity level to set calories, protein, carbs and fat
- Targets update automatically as your body weight changes
- You can still type in your own numbers anytime

## Oct 26 — New: Food tracker
- New Food tab to track calories, protein, carbs and fat each day
- Scan nutrition label: take a photo of the label and the numbers fill in automatically
- Always double-check the numbers against the label before adding
- Set your own daily goals, and re-add foods you eat often with one tap
- Food is included in Copy backup

## Oct 26 — More ways to rank every muscle
- Forearms: reverse curl, reverse/behind-the-back/cable wrist curls, dead hang, plate pinch
- Calves: leg press, dumbbell and single-leg calf raises
- Inner thighs: cable adduction, Copenhagen plank, dumbbell sumo squat
- Outer hips: cable abduction, side-lying leg raise, banded lateral walk, fire hydrant
- Lower back: good morning, weighted back extension, bird dog

## Oct 26 — Ranks are now harder
- Godlike now takes truly elite strength (top 5% of the scale)
- Higher standards for every rank above Diamond
- Sets over 12 reps no longer inflate your rank
- Your rank may drop a little — that's expected!
